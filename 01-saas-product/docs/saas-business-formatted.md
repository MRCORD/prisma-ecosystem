# PRD Builder – Business Rationale and Impact

## Executive Summary

The PRD (Product Requirements Document) Builder is a SaaS tool designed to revolutionize how product teams create, manage, and share product requirements. By leveraging AI-assisted drafting, standardized templates, and industry best practices, it transforms the traditionally tedious PRD creation process into an efficient, collaborative workflow that drives better product outcomes.

---

## Purpose and Vision

The PRD Builder addresses a critical pain point in product management: the time-consuming and often inconsistent process of documenting product requirements. Our solution aims to:

- **Simplify** the requirements capture process
- **Standardize** documentation across teams and organizations
- **Accelerate** product discovery and delivery cycles
- **Enhance** collaboration between cross-functional teams
- **Ensure** alignment between stakeholders at all levels
