## **Facilitation Guidelines**

### **Pre-Discussion Setup (5 minutes)**
- **Materials**: Each table receives printed question cards + note sheets
- **Instructions**: "Discutir por 15 minutos, preparar presentación de 3 minutos con ejemplos específicos"
- **Goal Communication**: "Busquen evidencia concreta y ejemplos reales, no opiniones generales"

### **Discussion Phase (15 minutes per table)**
- **Facilitator Role**: Circulate, ensure focus on specific examples and evidence
- **Key Prompts**: "¿Pueden dar un ejemplo específico?" / "¿Han visto esto en la práctica?"
- **Evidence Focus**: Push for concrete cases, measurable impacts, real scenarios

### **Presentation Strategy (3 minutes per table)**
- **PM Cycle Flow**: Discovery → Research → Strategy → Execution → Analytics → Communication
- **Domain Expertise**: Each table presents as domain specialists with specific AI evidence
- **Cumulative Impact**: Each domain builds on previous, showing AI transformation across entire PM workflow
- **Moderator Synthesis**: "Notice how AI is transforming every single aspect of PM work - not just one area"

### **Post-Presentation Transition (5 minutes)**
- **Synthesis**: "Estas experiencias confirman lo que todos intuíamos..."  
- **Bridge to Demo**: "Ahora vamos a ver exactamente cómo cerrar esa brecha que describieron"
- **Emotional Peak**: Maximum FOMO + desire for solution before your demo begins
