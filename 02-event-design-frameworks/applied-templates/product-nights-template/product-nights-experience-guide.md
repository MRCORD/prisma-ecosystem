# Product Nights - Curated After-Office Experience Guide

## Core Concept

**Product Nights** son espacios íntimos y curados para "líderes de producto senior de las +70 empresas más significativas de Perú" expandiéndose por LATAM. Diseñados como networking mensual presencial after-office experiences donde un experto reconocido "sparks the night" con knowledge de frontera que transforma perspectivas y conecta al mejor talento del ecosistema.

## Filosofía Central

### **"Spark the Night"**
- Un experto tier 1 enciende la noche con insights transformacionales
- No es networking casual - es growth intelectual acelerado
- Cada Product Night deja a los asistentes con herramientas mentales nuevas
- Focus en wisdom que solo se obtiene con experiencia de alto nivel

### **After-Office Premium**
- Timing optimizado: 6:30-9:30 PM (post work, pre dinner social)
- Ambiente sofisticado que refleja el nivel senior de la audiencia  
- Alcohol premium y food curation que facilita conversation depth
- Lighting y ambience diseñados para intimacy y focus

### **Curated Intimacy**
- 25-40 attendees máximo - suficiente para energy, pequeño para intimacy
- Selection criteria estricto: Director+ level en product/innovation
- Pre-screening para audience homogeneity y relevance
- No random networking - every person adds value to the collective experience

## Anatomía de una Product Night

### **Format Estructura (3 horas)**

#### **6:30 - 7:00 PM: Elegant Arrival**
- **Welcome drinks** con signature cocktails branded
- **Name badges** discretos con company/role para context
- **Soft networking** facilitado por hosts expertos
- **Ambient setup** con música, lighting, y visual coherence perfecta

#### **7:00 - 8:15 PM: Expert Spark Session**
- **Introduction contextualizada** del expert y su expertise
- **Core content** (45-60 min): deep insight sharing, no surface level
- **Interactive elements**: Q&A, small group discussions, frameworks sharing
- **Takeaway delivery**: Templates, checklists, o tools concretos

#### **8:15 - 8:30 PM: Transition & Reflection**
- **Networking break** con time para process lo escuchado
- **Small group discussions** (5-6 personas) sobre insights key
- **Facilitated mingling** con conversation starters específicos
- **Fresh drinks** y light food refresh

#### **8:30 - 9:30 PM: Deep Networking & Integration**
- **Structured conversations** con rotating small groups
- **Expert availability** para one-on-one questions
- **Business card exchange** exclusivo entre peers relevantes
- **Closing toast** con appreciation del expert y group

### **Content Categories Rotation**

#### **AI Product Leadership Series**
- **Expert Profile**: CPOs/VPs de AI-first companies
- **Content Focus**: Leading product teams en la era de AI
- **Takeaways**: Frameworks para AI product strategy, team evolution
- **Examples**: Ex-CPO de Cursor, Head of Product de Perplexity

#### **Scale-Up Mastery Series**  
- **Expert Profile**: Leaders que han scaling 1→10 en LATAM
- **Content Focus**: Tactical execution para hypergrowth
- **Takeaways**: Playbooks probados, process evolution, team building
- **Examples**: Ex-CPO de Rappi, VP Product de Nubank

#### **Strategic Innovation Series**
- **Expert Profile**: Corporate innovation leaders con results probados
- **Content Focus**: Innovation dentro de large organizations
- **Takeaways**: Frameworks para navigate corporate complexity
- **Examples**: Chief Innovation Officer de Santander, VP de BBVA

#### **Product Methodology Masters**
- **Expert Profile**: Creators de methodologies reconocidas globally
- **Content Focus**: Deep dive en frameworks avanzados
- **Takeaways**: Advanced techniques, certification opportunities
- **Examples**: Jobs-to-be-Done experts, Continuous Discovery leaders

#### **Founders Wisdom Series**
- **Expert Profile**: Founder-CPOs con exits exitosos
- **Content Focus**: 0→1 product building y strategic decisions
- **Takeaways**: War stories, decision frameworks, mindset shifts
- **Examples**: Founders de companies acquired, successful IPOs

## Expert Curation Standards

### **Tier 1 Criteria (Non-Negotiable)**

#### **Proven Track Record:**
- Leadership role en companies $50M+ valuation o equivalent corporate success
- Demonstrated results: growth metrics, successful launches, team scaling
- Industry recognition: speaking at tier 1 events, published frameworks
- Peer acknowledgment: recommended by other tier 1 product leaders

#### **Content Quality Standards:**
- **Zero sales pitch**: Pure educational value, no product/service promotion
- **Actionable insights**: Attendees leave con tools/frameworks aplicables immediately
- **Experience depth**: Content que solo comes from hands-on senior experience
- **Storytelling ability**: Can engage audience con narratives compelling

#### **Cultural Fit:**
- **Authenticity**: Genuine passion por sharing knowledge y growing others  
- **Humility**: Willing to share failures y lessons learned honestly
- **Accessibility**: Can connect con audience sin condescension
- **Energy match**: Understands the intimate, premium nature del format

### **Content Standards**

#### **Value Density Requirements:**
- **90% new insights**: Not content easily found en blogs o general talks
- **Frameworks sharing**: Templates, checklists, tools shared con audience
- **Interactive participation**: Q&A, exercises, group discussions
- **Follow-up resources**: Additional materials provided post-event

#### **Storytelling Structure:**
- **Context setting**: Current landscape/challenges en product management
- **Personal journey**: How expert learned these insights through experience
- **Framework reveal**: Tools/methodologies developed through experience  
- **Practical application**: How audience can implement insights immediately
- **Q&A depth**: Thoughtful responses que add value beyond initial content

## Audience Curation

### **Target Profile: Senior Product Leaders**

#### **Primary Audience:**
- **Director of Product** o higher at growth companies
- **VP Product/CPO** at scale-ups ($10M+ revenue)
- **Senior Product Manager** con 8+ years experience
- **Founders** con product responsibility at funded startups

#### **Secondary Audience:**
- **Innovation Leaders** at corporations (Manager+ level)
- **Product Consultants** con senior client base  
- **Angel Investors** con product background
- **Former Product Leaders** now en other roles (CTO, CEO)

### **Application & Selection Process**

#### **Application Requirements:**
- **LinkedIn profile** con clear senior product experience
- **Brief application**: Current role, company, why interested
- **Reference check**: Connection to previous attendee o community member
- **Capacity management**: Accept based on audience mix optimization

#### **Selection Criteria:**
- **Seniority verification**: Confirmed Director+ level responsibility
- **Company credibility**: Legitimate business con traction/funding
- **Cultural fit**: Professional profile aligned con premium experience
- **Network value**: Adds value to collective group dynamic

## Experience Optimization

### **Venue Requirements**

#### **Atmosphere Essentials:**
- **Premium location**: Reflects audience seniority (rooftop, private club, boutique space)
- **Intimate capacity**: 25-35 optimal, 40 maximum for conversation quality
- **Technical setup**: Excellent acoustics, projector/screen availability
- **Networking zones**: Multiple spaces para different conversation types

#### **Ambience Design:**
- **Lighting premium**: Warm, sophisticated, conducive to focus y conversation
- **Sound management**: Background music appropriate, excellent acoustic
- **Visual coherence**: Prisma branding integrated elegantly, not overwhelming
- **Comfort optimization**: Seating arrangement optimized para attention y networking

### **Food & Beverage Curation**

#### **Alcohol Selection:**
- **Signature cocktails** con Prisma theming sutil
- **Premium wine** selection (2-3 options curadas)
- **Craft beer** local options para variety
- **Non-alcoholic** options sophisticated (not just soft drinks)

#### **Food Strategy:**
- **Light appetizers** que no interfieren con conversation
- **Easy-to-eat** options que don't require sitting down
- **Quality over quantity**: Fewer options but premium execution
- **Dietary considerations**: Vegetarian/vegan options always available

### **Technology Integration**

#### **Registration & Check-in:**
- **Simple RSVP** process with calendar integration
- **Digital check-in** con QR codes para efficiency
- **Name tags** with role/company discretely displayed
- **Contact sharing** facilitation (opt-in basis)

#### **Content Capture:**
- **Audio recording** (con permission) para podcast/content repurposing
- **Key quotes** capture para social media y follow-up
- **Photo documentation** professional pero no intrusive
- **Template sharing** digital durante o post event
