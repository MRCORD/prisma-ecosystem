# Expert Speaker Curation & Content Strategy Guide

## Curation Philosophy: Value Density Above All

**Core Principle**: Every expert must deliver insights que no están available elsewhere - knowledge que only comes from senior hands-on experience building and scaling products at the highest level.

### **The "Spark Test"**
Before confirming any expert, they must pass the "Spark Test":
- **Unique Perspective**: Do they have insights unavailable en blogs, podcasts, or general conferences?
- **Proven Results**: Can they show concrete outcomes from applying their expertise?
- **Teaching Ability**: Can they distill complex experience into actionable frameworks?
- **Authentic Stories**: Do they share real failures and lessons honestly?

## Content Framework Standards

### **The Value Density Formula**

#### **90/10 Rule**: 90% unique insights, 10% context setting
- **No generic content**: Nothing que audience could find en public resources
- **Experience-driven**: Insights que only come from doing the work at scale
- **Tactical depth**: Specific frameworks, tools, and processes shared
- **Honest storytelling**: Real failures and lessons learned openly shared

#### **Actionable Takeaway Guarantee**
Every expert session must deliver:
- **Framework/Template**: Downloadable tool que attendees can use immediately
- **Checklists**: Step-by-step guides para implementation
- **Decision Trees**: Clear criteria para common product decisions
- **War Stories**: Specific examples with tactical details

### **Content Structure Templates**

#### **For Product Nights (75 minutes)**

##### **Opening Hook (5 min)**
- **Personal story** que sets up the learning journey
- **Stakes establishment**: Why this matters for senior product leaders
- **Promise delivery**: Exactly what attendees will learn and take away

##### **Context & Framework (20 min)**
- **Industry landscape**: Current state and key challenges
- **Personal journey**: How expert developed their approach
- **Core framework**: The methodology/system they've developed

##### **Deep Dive Application (30 min)**
- **Case study walkthrough**: Real example con specific metrics
- **Framework application**: Step-by-step process demonstration
- **Common pitfalls**: What goes wrong and how to avoid it

##### **Interactive Q&A (15 min)**
- **Scenario planning**: Audience situations con expert guidance
- **Advanced applications**: Sophisticated use cases discussion
- **Resource sharing**: Additional tools and templates

##### **Closing & Takeaways (5 min)**
- **Summary consolidation**: Key insights reinforcement
- **Action items**: What to do first on Monday morning
- **Follow-up resources**: Where to go deeper

#### **For World Product Day (45-60 minutes)**

##### **Keynote Structure**
- **Inspiration Opening** (10 min): Why this moment en product management matters
- **Framework Deep-Dive** (25 min): Core methodology con examples
- **Interactive Elements** (10 min): Audience participation o exercises
- **Call-to-Action Close** (10 min): How to apply these insights immediately

##### **Panel Structure**
- **Question-driven** format con specific scenarios
- **Real-time problem solving** con audience challenges
- **Framework comparison** between panelists' approaches
- **Tactical advice** con specific recommendations

## Expert Outreach & Acquisition Strategy

### **Relationship Building Approach**

#### **Warm Introduction Path (Preferred)**
- **Mutual connections**: Leverage existing network para introductions
- **Previous speakers**: Ask current experts para referrals
- **Community members**: Use attendee networks para connections
- **Industry events**: Meet experts at tier 1 conferences

#### **Direct Outreach Strategy**
- **Value-first approach**: Lead with what we offer them (audience, content amplification)
- **Specific ask**: Clear on format, time commitment, and expectations
- **Social proof**: Share previous expert feedback and community strength
- **Long-term relationship**: Position as ongoing partnership, not one-time ask

- **Long-term relationship**: Ongoing updates on community growth and impact
- **Referral facilitation**: Introductions to other speaking opportunities

## Quality Control & Feedback Loop

### **Speaker Vetting Process**

#### **Application Review**
- **Track record verification**: LinkedIn, company verification, public speaking history
- **Content quality assessment**: Previous talks, written content, framework originality
- **Cultural fit evaluation**: Alignment con premium community values
- **Reference checks**: Conversations con colleagues or previous event organizers

#### **Pre-Event Content Review**
- **Framework review**: Ensure uniqueness and tactical value
- **Audience relevance**: Verify content matches senior product leader needs
- **Takeaway verification**: Confirm downloadable resources are valuable
- **Story authenticity**: Validate case studies and examples are real

### **Post-Event Optimization**

#### **Immediate Feedback Collection**
- **Attendee survey**: Content quality, speaker effectiveness, format satisfaction
- **Expert debrief**: Speaker experience, audience engagement, improvement suggestions
- **Venue feedback**: Logistics, atmosphere, technical setup effectiveness
- **Host reflection**: Overall experience quality and community building impact

#### **Continuous Improvement Process**
- **Monthly review**: Analyze feedback trends and identify improvement areas
- **Speaker database**: Track performance metrics para future booking decisions
- **Format evolution**: Adjust structure based on what drives highest engagement
- **Community input**: Regular surveys on desired topics and expert types

#### **Expert Experience KPIs**
