## **INVESTIGACIÓN DE MERCADO: BENCHMARK DE EVENTOS SIMILARES**

### **Eventos Product Management Internacionales - Pricing 2024**

#### **ProductCon (Product School) - Referencia Global**
- **Alcance**: Largest PM conference worldwide (4 events/year)  
- **Locations**: San Francisco, New York, London, Online
- **Pricing**: **GRATIS** (modelo freemium con upsells)
- **Startup Access**: $1,599 para founders (<$40M ARR)
- **Modelo**: Lead generation masivo → conversión en bootcamps/certificaciones

#### **Mind the Product - Premium Global**
- **Alcance**: Miles de attendees, SF + London
- **Pricing**: $500-$700 (early bird) hasta $1,500+ (premium tiers)
- **Target**: Senior PMs, C-level executives
- **Modelo**: Premium content + networking exclusivo

#### **La Product Conf (Paris) - European Premium**
- **Pricing**: $614 early bird, $700+ standard  
- **Focus**: European product leadership
- **Modelo**: Premium positioning con speakers top-tier

#### **Front Conference - Technical Premium**
- **Pricing**: $500-$700 conference, $1,349-$1,799 workshop series
- **Target**: Technical product leaders
- **Modelo**: Conference + workshops intensivos

### **Eventos LATAM Benchmark**

#### **ProductCamp Brazil - Regional Leader**  
- **Alcance**: Biggest PM conference en LATAM (1,800+ attendees 2023)
- **Organizer**: PM3  
- **Target**: LATAM Spanish/Portuguese speakers
- **Pricing**: No disponible públicamente (likely $200-$400 range)

#### **POMS & MIT-SCALE LAC (Colombia)**
- **Location**: Cartagena de Indias, Colombia  
- **Academic Focus**: Operations/supply chain management
- **Pricing**: $25 students + $5 membership, professionals con descuentos hasta $170
- **Modelo**: Academic-corporate hybrid

### **Key Insights del Mercado**

✅ **Gap de Mercado Identificado**: No existe un evento premium de Product Management en LATAM con focus en AI/future of work  
✅ **Pricing Range**: $250-$1,500+ dependiendo del nivel de exclusividad  
✅ **Modelo Exitoso**: Premium positioning + networking exclusivo + content no-disponible elsewhere  
✅ **LATAM Opportunity**: Market underserved para eventos premium en español/portugués
